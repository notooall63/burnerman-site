What Is a Burner Phone?

A burner phone is a prepaid mobile phone that you use temporarily — usually for days or weeks — instead of your everyday phone, so your calls, messages, and searches cannot easily be tied back to your identity. It is not a special kind of phone. It is an ordinary smartphone used in a way that leaves no account, billing record, or personal trail attached to you.

How it works

How does a burner phone work?

A burner phone works the same way any smartphone works, with three differences:

  • No account in your name. The phone is bought and set up without your name, your credit card, or your existing accounts attached to it.
  • Prepaid service, no contract. Service is paid for in advance, usually for a short period, and there is no monthly bill linking you to the number.
  • Short life, then gone. When you are done, the phone is wiped and recycled — not kept, traded in, or passed along with your data still on it.

One thing matters as much as the phone itself: how you use it. Privacy is a combination of technology and operational discipline. A phone that is not tied to you only stays that way if you follow the rules that keep it that way — which is why Burner Man ships every phone with usage guidelines, not just hardware.

The key question

Why can't I just buy a prepaid phone at Amazon, Walmart, or Target?

Technically, you could — if you want a third party to be able to trace the purchase, and everything you do on the phone, back to you. Retailers are required to maintain records of every sale: the paper receipt and payment information, store video surveillance, and for online orders, your IP address, metadata, and payment footprint.

Burner Man is a service that breaks that chain while staying within the law. He purchases the phone in person using his own payment methods, so no record connects you to the device — no card trail, no store camera, no account.

Legality

Is a burner phone legal?

Yes. There is nothing inherently illegal about acquiring or using a phone that is not tied to your identity. How you use it is your responsibility, and the service is not intended for malicious or criminal activity.

Common questions

Questions people ask

How long should you keep a burner phone?

Short rotations work best. The usage guidelines recommend never keeping a single device for more than 2 to 4 weeks, because long-term use gives observers more chances to connect the device to you. The renewal service exists for exactly this reason: a freshly randomized device at your preferred interval.

Can a burner phone be traced?

No phone is magic. Phones exchange unique identifiers with cell towers even when idle, which is why the usage guidelines matter: keep the phone in its Faraday pouch when not in use, never power it on near your home, workplace, or routine locations, never use it to contact anyone who knows you, and have it delivered to a neutral parcel locker or third-party address — never a location registered in your name. Failing to follow the rules does not automatically compromise your privacy, but it does void the guaranteed privacy standard the system offers.
