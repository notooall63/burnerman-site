What Is a Faraday Pouch, and Why Does It Matter?

A Faraday pouch is a pouch lined with conductive material that physically blocks all radio signals from reaching the phone inside — or leaving it. No cellular, no Wi-Fi, no Bluetooth, no GPS. While the phone is sealed inside, it cannot exchange identifiers with cell towers, cannot be located by its signal, and cannot receive or send anything. It is the simplest, most reliable privacy tool in the whole system, and every Burner Man phone ships with one.

How it works

Why a pouch, and not just a setting?

Your phone talks to the world through radios, and those radios talk even when you are not using the phone. A phone exchanges unique hardware identifiers with surrounding cell towers whenever its radios are active — including when the phone is asleep. That background chatter is what lets a network know a device exists, where it is, and where it has been.

A Faraday pouch stops that chatter physically. The conductive lining forms a shield around the phone — the same principle as a Faraday cage — so signals cannot pass in either direction. It does not depend on software, on a setting, or on the phone behaving the way you expect. That is exactly why the rule is pouch-first: airplane mode is a software switch on a device full of software you do not control, while a pouch is physics.

The rule

How to use it right

The pouch only works if it is used the way it is meant to be used:

  • Sealed, whenever the phone is not actively in use. The phone goes back in the pouch the moment you are done — not later, not when convenient. A phone sitting outside the pouch is a phone talking to towers.
  • Fully closed. The phone must be completely inside with the pouch sealed shut. A half-open pouch leaks signal, which defeats the purpose.
  • Power on only for as long as needed. The usage guidelines recommend powering on only for the exact duration required to send or receive what you need — ideally under 60 seconds — then powering down and returning the phone to its sealed pouch.
  • Never carry it alongside your personal devices. Do not carry the pouch next to your personal smartphone, smartwatch, Bluetooth accessories, or inside a connected vehicle. Co-location systems can mathematically link two devices carried together to the same person, pouch or not, once the burner comes out.

Honest limits

What a pouch cannot do

A pouch is not magic either. It does not wipe the phone, it does not make the phone itself anonymous, and it does nothing if someone physically takes the phone in hand. The moment you take the phone out and power it on, it can communicate again — which is why the pouch is paired with the other rules: short rotations, varied locations, and never contacting people who know you. The pouch covers the time the phone is idle. The rules cover the time it is not.

Common questions

Questions people ask

How do I know my pouch works?

Seal the phone inside, fully closed, then try calling it from another phone. If the pouch is working, the call should not connect — the phone cannot be reached while sealed. If it rings, the pouch is not fully closed or is damaged, and it needs to be replaced.

Can I use any bag or case instead?

No. An ordinary bag, pocket, or case does nothing to radio signals. Only a pouch with conductive Faraday lining blocks them. That is why one is included with every Burner Man phone rather than left to chance.

Do I need the pouch if I power the phone off?

Yes — the rule is pouch whenever the phone is not actively in use, powered off or not. Powering down is good practice on top of the pouch, not a replacement for it. Belt and suspenders is the point.
