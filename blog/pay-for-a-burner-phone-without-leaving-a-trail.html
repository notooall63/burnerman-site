How Do I Pay for a Burner Phone Without Leaving a Trail?

It depends on how far you want to go. There are three ways to pay, from most convenient to most private: card through Square, an order by phone or email, or Monero. Each one leaves a different record — and none of them gives Burner Man your card details, because the company never sees or stores them.

Option 1

Card via Square — the convenient route

Order through the Square checkout on the site. Square handles the payment securely, and Burner Man Co. does not see or store your card details. That is the honest limit of this option, though: a card payment still leaves a record with your bank and the payment processor, even though it leaves nothing with Burner Man. If your concern is keeping the purchase away from the company selling you the phone, this does that. If your concern is that no financial record of the purchase exists anywhere, it does not go far enough — use Monero instead.

Option 2

Phone or email order — you pick the channel

Order by phone at (732) 633-0200, or by email. Mail orders are also available on request via email, if you prefer a paper trail you control. The point of offering several channels is that you choose the one that fits your risk profile — a phone call, an email thread, or a mailed order each leave different kinds of records, and you decide which tradeoff you are comfortable with.

Option 3

Monero — the fully private rail

For users who require a fully privacy-preserving payment rail, Burner Man Co. accepts Monero (XMR). Monero is built for this: unlike most cryptocurrencies, it obscures the sender, the receiver, and the amount of each transaction by design, and it carries low fees. Combined with the anonymizing process, it adds another layer of protection to your communications. Include your shipping details in an agreed secure channel, and the Monero address and QR code are on the payment section of the homepage.

After payment

What does Burner Man keep on file?

Almost nothing. The company keeps no CRM, no analytics profile, and no customer database. Only the monetary amount of a transaction is logged, for tax compliance. Shipping information and other order details are deleted and purged once they are no longer operationally necessary. If a returned phone is in good working condition, the email address you provide can receive a discount on a future purchase — and that is the only record kept, only until it is redeemed.

Common questions

Questions people ask

Does Burner Man see my card number?

No. Card payments go through Square's secure checkout. Burner Man Co. does not see or store your card details at any point.

Is Monero hard to use?

It takes a little setup the first time — a Monero wallet and one transfer — and after that it works like any other payment, with low fees. If you have never used it, the first transaction is the learning curve; the second one is routine.

Which payment method should I choose?

Match it to your concern. If you want convenience and are comfortable with a bank record existing, use Square. If you want no financial record tying you to the purchase at all, use Monero. If you want to control the channel itself, order by phone, email, or mail.

Order online via Square, by phone at (732) 633-0200, or by email. Monero (XMR) is also accepted for users who require a fully privacy-preserving payment rail.
