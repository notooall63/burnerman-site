Do Burner Phones Work Internationally?

Yes — with the international plan. Burner Man phones can be provisioned with 30 days of U.S. plus international service to many countries, and they arrive ready with worldwide mobile data and Wi-Fi capability. Which countries are covered and how much data is included depends on the plan, so confirm your destination before you order.

How it works abroad

No setup at your destination

The phone arrives ready — service already applied, temporary accounts already created, VoIP tools already installed. There is nothing to configure when you land. The phone's worldwide mobile data and Wi-Fi capability mean it can communicate over cellular data where the plan covers it, and over Wi-Fi through its VoIP tools where there is internet access. The temporary accounts work the same abroad as they do at home, because they were never tied to you in the first place.

Before you travel

What to check, and what still applies

  • Confirm your destination. "Many countries" does not mean every country. Coverage and data allotments vary, so email with your destination before ordering to confirm what is available.
  • The rules travel with you. Everything in the usage guidelines applies abroad — arguably more so. Airports and border crossings are high-attention places where devices get extra scrutiny, so the pouch rule, the rotation rule, and the never-carry-it-with-your-personal-phone rule matter more there, not less.
  • Do not bridge it to home. Do not connect the phone to your home Wi-Fi before you leave or after you return, and do not use it to contact people who know you. A phone that touches your home network or your personal contacts is no longer separate from your identity, no matter where it traveled.
  • Plan the end of the trip. The 30-day window and the 2-to-4-week rotation still apply abroad. If your trip runs longer, arrange renewal with a fresh device rather than stretching one phone past its lifespan.

Common questions

Questions people ask

Can I use it in any country?

In many countries, not all. International service covers a wide list of destinations, but coverage is not universal. Email with your destination before ordering and Burner Man Co. will confirm what is available for that trip.

What if I need more than 30 days?

Email for current options and pricing — more time and data are available on request. The renewal system also applies: fresh devices at intervals of 1, 2, or 4 weeks keep each phone's lifespan short and its attribution risk low, abroad or at home.

Does traveling with it create extra risk?

Travel adds attention, not new kinds of risk. Airports, border crossings, and unfamiliar networks are places where devices are looked at more closely. The same discipline that protects you at home — pouch sealed when idle, varied locations, no personal contacts, no personal phone carried alongside — is what protects you abroad. The phone does not change at the border; your discipline should not either.
