Usage

Private‑communications usage guidelines

To ensure that communications conveyed through a smartphone provided by Burner Man remain completely private and non‑attributable to your personal identity, you must strictly observe the following operational security (OPSEC) protocol. Because all digital communications and radio‑frequency signals are recorded by global telecommunications networks, true privacy requires preventing the hardware, network traffic, and physical location of your device from ever being linked to your real‑world identity.

  1. Contact & social graph isolation
    Never use your Burner Man device to contact anyone who knows you by voice, name, or personal association. Do not contact any phone number, email address, or handle present in your personal address book or social media accounts. If you must communicate with an associate, ensure they are also using a device secured through Burner Man and following these exact usage guidelines. Avoid using personal idioms, unique slang, regional jargon, or distinct writing cadences that could link your messages to your writing style.
  2. Digital & account partitioning
    Never log into, access, or interact with any email account, social media profile, cloud storage, app store, or online service that has ever been accessed from a non‑anonymous device or account associated with you. Do not connect your device to home Wi‑Fi, workplace Wi‑Fi, or public access points near locations associated with your daily routine. Use cellular data only or untethered public access points away from your home base. Never download files, applications, media, or software that contain personal identifiers, metadata, or account links.
  3. Hardware & radio‑frequency (RF) management
    Always store the device inside the provided Faraday signal‑blocking pouch whenever it is not actively in use. Phones continuously exchange unique hardware identifiers with surrounding cell towers, even when asleep or in airplane mode; the Faraday pouch physically blocks all radio signals and background location pings. Never carry your Burner Man device alongside your personal smartphone, smartwatch, Bluetooth accessories, or inside a connected vehicle. Telecommunications providers use automated co‑location algorithms that can mathematically link both devices to the same person. Power on your device only for the exact duration required to send or receive necessary communications (ideally under 60 seconds), then immediately power down the device and return it to its sealed Faraday pouch.
  4. Location & spatial security
    Never turn on, unshield, or use your device within 1 to 5 miles of your residence, workplace, or routine locations. Always vary the physical location where you unshield and power on your device; never activate the device in the exact same spot, intersection, or sector twice. Do not power on or use the device while traveling along your primary daily commute routes.
  5. Device lifespan, rotation & disposal
    Never retain any single Burner Man device for more than 2 to 4 weeks. Short rotation intervals break long‑term pattern‑of‑life mapping. Even if cellular data expires, do not continue using the phone over Wi‑Fi in perpetuity. Return the hardware to Burner Man or dispose of it through approved channels. To maintain continuous, guaranteed private communications, utilize Burner Man’s automatic renewal service to receive a freshly randomized device at your preferred interval (1, 2, or 4 weeks).
  6. Physical retrieval & chain of custody
    Ensure your Burner Man shipment is delivered to a neutral parcel locker, dead drop, or third‑party address—never directly to a location registered in your name. When retrieving packages from drop points, wear non‑descript clothing that covers identifiable physical features to neutralize CCTV and license plate tracking systems.

Using Burner Man provides an anonymizing phone pool and data‑less transaction structure that removes the paper trail between your identity and the device. However, privacy is a combination of technology and operational discipline. Adhering strictly to every rule above removes the paper, digital, hardware, and physical‑location trails, providing the highest possible standard of privacy and non‑attributability. Failure to observe these rules does not automatically compromise your privacy, but it voids the guaranteed privacy standard offered by the Burner Man system.
